A softer side
of Saudi.

Abha is the capital of the Asir Region in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and one of the most prominent cultural and tourist centers in the Sarawat Mountains. It is situated at a high altitude that grants it a mild climate, making it a primary destination for those seeking nature and cool weather.

01 · ABHA'S NATURE

Where clouds
take the trail.

Abha's nature is characterized by a unique geography that combines majestic mountain peaks, dense green slopes, and flowing plains. Situated at a high altitude, the city enjoys a mild climate and rich vegetation dominated by ancient juniper trees, alongside the constant fog and clouds that blanket its highlands.

02 · HERITAGE & ARCHITECTURE

Stories built
in stone.

The traditional architecture in Abha and the Asir region stands out as one of the most significant and distinctive architectural identities in the Arabian Peninsula. This architecture primarily relies on total harmony with the surrounding mountainous environment and changeable climate, utilizing local natural resources (stone, mud, and juniper wood) to achieve the highest standards of durability and thermal insulation.
